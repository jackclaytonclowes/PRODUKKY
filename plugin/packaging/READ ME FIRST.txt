FRACTURE — multi-band multi-fx distortion
=========================================

A three-band distortion unit: two serial drive stages per band with fourteen
shaper modes, a bit crusher, a feedback loop, a resonant filter, and two LFOs
plus an envelope follower patched through a six-slot modulation matrix.

It installs as an Audio Unit (Logic, GarageBand, Live) and a VST3 (Live, Reaper,
Cubase, Bitwig), and there is a standalone app if you just want to play with it.


INSTALLING
----------

1. Double-click "Install FRACTURE.command".

2. macOS will probably refuse the first time — "cannot be opened because it is
   from an unidentified developer". That is expected: this is a personal build,
   not something bought from the App Store, so Apple has nothing to check it
   against. Right-click the file, choose Open, then Open again in the dialog.

3. Terminal opens, the installer copies the plugins into your own plug-in
   folders, and then runs Apple's own validator on the Audio Unit. If it says
   PASSED, you are done.

4. Drag FRACTURE.app to Applications if you want the standalone version.

Restart Logic afterwards. FRACTURE appears under Audio Units → Fracture →
Distortion. If it does not show up, quit Logic, open Terminal and run:

    killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar

then start Logic again.


WHAT IT INSTALLS, AND WHERE
---------------------------

    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/FRACTURE.component
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/FRACTURE.vst3

Nothing else is touched. No background processes, no login items, no network
access of any kind — the plugin never opens a connection. To uninstall, delete
those two files.


BEFORE YOU LOAD IT ON SOMETHING YOU CARE ABOUT
----------------------------------------------

This is an early build that has been tested by machine, not by ear: the maths is
checked against 9,000-odd reference points and the whole thing is proven not to
produce silence, NaNs or runaway levels, but nobody has sat and listened to it in
a session yet. That is what you are being asked to do.

Save your project first. Start with the Safety clip on (it is on by default, and
it hard-limits the output). Watch out for the feedback control, which is meant
to be unruly.


WHAT WOULD BE USEFUL TO HEAR BACK
---------------------------------

  – does anything crackle, zipper or click when you move a knob quickly?
  – does the crush section alias in a way that sounds broken rather than nasty?
  – does a bounce sound the same as playback?
  – does the interface make sense without anyone explaining it?
  – which modes do you actually reach for, and which never get used?

Thanks for trying it.
