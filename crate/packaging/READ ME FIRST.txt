CRATE — twelve-bit drum processor
=================================

A drum bus processor after the sound of the late-80s sampling boxes: a 26.04 kHz
sample clock, companded twelve-bit conversion, a four-pole filter, the noise
floor of the record it came off, and a swing section that moves the off-beats
against the host grid.

It installs as an Audio Unit (Logic, GarageBand, Live) and a VST3 (Live, Reaper,
Cubase, Bitwig), and there is a standalone app for playing with it.


INSTALLING
----------

1. Double-click "Install CRATE.command".

2. macOS will probably refuse the first time — "cannot be opened because it is
   from an unidentified developer". That is expected: this is a personal build,
   not something bought from the App Store, so Apple has nothing to check it
   against. Right-click the file, choose Open, then Open again in the dialog.

3. Terminal opens, the installer copies the plugins into your own plug-in
   folders, and then runs Apple's own validator on the Audio Unit. If it says
   PASSED, you are done.

Restart Logic afterwards. CRATE appears under Audio Units → Crate → Distortion.
If it does not show up, quit Logic, open Terminal and run:

    killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar

then start Logic again.


TWO THINGS THAT WILL LOOK LIKE BUGS AND ARE NOT
-----------------------------------------------

SWING NEEDS THE TRANSPORT RUNNING. The grid comes from the host, so with the
transport stopped the swing knob does nothing at all. The panel says so.

IT REPORTS LATENCY, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. The whole plugin sits behind a fixed
delay so that push can pull hits EARLIER as well as later. Your DAW compensates
for it automatically. If you have delay compensation switched off, everything
through this plugin will sit late by about 120 ms.


WHAT IT INSTALLS, AND WHERE
---------------------------

    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/CRATE.component
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/CRATE.vst3

Nothing else is touched, and the plugin makes no network connections of any
kind. To uninstall, delete those two files.


BEFORE YOU PUT IT ON SOMETHING THAT MATTERS
-------------------------------------------

This is an early build that has been measured, not listened to: the converter,
the filter and the swing offsets are all verified against the numbers they claim
on the panel, but nobody has yet sat and made a beat with it. That is the job.

Save your project first, and leave Safety clip on to start with.


WHAT WOULD BE USEFUL TO HEAR BACK
---------------------------------

  – does the swing feel like swing, or like a delay? Try it on a loop with a
    long tail (a rhodes chord under the drums) where the smearing is worst
  – does Tune do what you would expect a pitched-down sample to do?
  – is Dust usable, or is it a novelty?
  – does anything crackle or click when you move a knob while it plays?
  – what is missing before you would reach for it instead of a stock plugin?

Thanks for trying it.
