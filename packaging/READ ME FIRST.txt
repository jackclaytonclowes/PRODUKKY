FRACTURE and CRATE
==================

Two audio plugins, in one disk image.

FRACTURE — multi-band multi-fx distortion. Three bands, two serial drive stages
each with fifteen shaper modes (one of them a table of harmonics you draw), a bit crusher, a feedback loop, a resonant
filter, two LFOs and an envelope follower into a six-slot modulation matrix.

CRATE — twelve-bit drum processor. A 26.04 kHz sample clock, companded twelve-bit
conversion, a four-pole filter, the noise floor of the record the break came off,
and a swing section that pulls the off-beats late against the host's grid.

Both install as Audio Units (Logic, GarageBand, Live) and VST3 (Live, Reaper,
Cubase, Bitwig). Standalone apps are in the "Standalone apps" folder if you want
to play without opening a DAW.


INSTALLING
----------

1. Double-click "Install both plugins.command".

2. macOS will probably refuse the first time — "cannot be opened because it is
   from an unidentified developer". That is expected: these are personal builds,
   not something bought from the App Store, so Apple has nothing to check them
   against. Right-click the file, choose Open, then Open again in the dialog.

3. Terminal opens, the installer copies both plugins into your own plug-in
   folders, and then runs Apple's own validator on each Audio Unit. Two passes
   and you are done.

Use the installer rather than dragging the plugins into the plug-in folders
yourself. A dragged copy keeps the mark macOS puts on everything downloaded,
and Logic then refuses it ("could not be opened", or a validation failure). The
installer clears that mark and re-signs each plugin where it lands. If you have
already dragged them in, quit Logic, open Terminal and run:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/*.component ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/*.vst3
    killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar

Restart Logic afterwards. They appear under Audio Units → Fracture → Distortion
and Audio Units → Crate → Distortion. If they do not show up, quit Logic, open
Terminal and run:

    killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar

then start Logic again.


TWO THINGS ABOUT CRATE THAT LOOK LIKE BUGS AND ARE NOT
-------------------------------------------------------

SWING NEEDS THE TRANSPORT RUNNING. The grid comes from the host, so with
playback stopped the swing knob does nothing at all.

IT REPORTS LATENCY ON PURPOSE. The whole plugin sits behind a fixed delay (about
120 ms) so Push can pull hits EARLIER as well as later. Your DAW compensates for
it automatically — unless you have delay compensation switched off, in which case
everything through it will sit late.


WHAT GETS INSTALLED, AND WHERE
------------------------------

    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/FRACTURE.component
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/CRATE.component
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/FRACTURE.vst3
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/CRATE.vst3

Nothing else is touched. No background processes, no login items, and neither
plugin opens a network connection of any kind. To uninstall, delete those four.


BEFORE YOU PUT THEM ON SOMETHING THAT MATTERS
----------------------------------------------

Both are early builds that have been measured rather than listened to. The
converter, the filter and the swing offsets are all verified against the numbers
printed on the panels, and neither plugin can produce silence, NaNs or runaway
levels — but nobody has sat and made a record with either yet.

Save your project first. Leave Safety clip on to start with; it hard-limits the
output on both.
