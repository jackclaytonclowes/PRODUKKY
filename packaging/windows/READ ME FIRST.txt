FRACTURE and CRATE for Windows
==============================

Two audio plugins, as VST3 for 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.

FRACTURE — multi-band multi-fx distortion. Three bands, two serial drive stages
each with fifteen shaper modes (one of them a table of harmonics you draw), a bit
crusher, a feedback loop, a resonant filter, two LFOs and an envelope follower
into a six-slot modulation matrix.

CRATE — twelve-bit drum processor. A 26.04 kHz sample clock, companded twelve-bit
conversion, a four-pole filter, the noise floor of the record the break came off,
and a swing section that pulls the off-beats late against the host's grid.

They load in any 64-bit VST3 host: Ableton Live 12 (and 10.1 or later), Reaper,
Cubase, Bitwig, FL Studio, Studio One. Standalone apps are included if you want
to play without opening a DAW.


INSTALLING
----------

With the installer (Fracture-and-Crate-...-Windows-Setup.exe):

1. Run it. Windows SmartScreen will probably say "Windows protected your PC".
   That is expected: these are unsigned personal builds, so Microsoft has no
   publisher to check them against. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

2. It installs both VST3s into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, the folder
   every Windows host scans, and the standalone apps into Program Files with a
   Start menu entry each.

From the zip instead: copy FRACTURE.vst3 and CRATE.vst3 (each is a folder; copy
the whole folder) from "VST3" into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Windows
asks for administrator permission to write there.


ABLETON LIVE 12
---------------

1. Open Live, then Options > Settings (Ctrl+,) > Plug-Ins.
2. Turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders".
3. Press "Rescan". Hold Alt while pressing it if Live had already scanned an
   older build and does not pick up the new one.
4. In the browser, under Plug-Ins > VST3, they appear as Fracture > FRACTURE
   and Crate > CRATE. Drag one onto an audio track.

Sidechain (FRACTURE's envelope follower can listen to another track): unfold
the plug-in device (the small triangle in its title bar) to reach its Sidechain
section, turn it on, and pick the kick's track under "Audio From". Then set Follows to
Sidechain in FRACTURE's Modulation panel.

Live compensates for both plugins' latency automatically. Keep
Options > Delay Compensation on.


TWO THINGS ABOUT CRATE THAT LOOK LIKE BUGS AND ARE NOT
-------------------------------------------------------

SWING NEEDS THE TRANSPORT RUNNING. The grid comes from the host, so with
playback stopped the swing knob does nothing at all.

IT REPORTS LATENCY ON PURPOSE. The whole plugin sits behind a fixed delay (about
120 ms) so Push can pull hits EARLIER as well as later. Your DAW compensates for
it automatically, unless you have delay compensation switched off, in which case
everything through it will sit late.


WHAT GETS INSTALLED, AND WHERE
------------------------------

    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\FRACTURE.vst3
    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\CRATE.vst3
    C:\Program Files\Fracture and Crate\           (the standalone apps)

Your own presets are saved in Documents\FRACTURE\Presets and
Documents\CRATE\Presets, and the uninstaller leaves them alone. No background
processes, nothing at login, and neither plugin opens a network connection of
any kind. Uninstall from Settings > Apps > FRACTURE and CRATE.


BEFORE YOU PUT THEM ON SOMETHING THAT MATTERS
----------------------------------------------

Both are early builds that have been measured rather than listened to. The
converter, the filter and the swing offsets are all verified against the numbers
printed on the panels, and neither plugin can produce silence, NaNs or runaway
levels, but nobody has sat and made a record with either yet. The Windows builds
are checked by machine (the DSP tests and pluginval) and have not yet been
opened in Live by a person.

Save your project first. Leave Safety clip on to start with; it hard-limits the
output on both.
